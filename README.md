# Analysis and Redesign of an Industrial Servo Brake
Group project focused on analyzing, modeling, and improving a servo-brake used in industrial mills  to control and stop large rotating masses.

## Description

Group project focused on analyzing, modeling, and improving a **servo-brake** used in industrial mills (cement and mining sectors) to control and stop large rotating masses. Starting from the manufacturer's technical drawings, we studied how each part works, modeled the full assembly in SolidWorks, ran structural simulations, and designed a **custom test bench** to validate the components' resistance under real load conditions.

The project combines knowledge from **Strength of Materials, Technical Drawing, 3D CAD (SolidWorks), and Industrial Design**.

## Objectives

- Analyze the operation of an industrial servo-brake and its key components.
- Model the complete assembly in SolidWorks (7 parts + assembly).
- Simulate structural behavior (Von Mises stress, safety factor) using SolidWorks Simulation.
- Design and model a test bench able to subject the system's rod to **buckling, torsion, and bending**.
- Document the process through technical drawings, a written report, and assembly/test videos.

### Test bench

Custom design consisting of a **fixed jaw** and a **movable jaw** (mounted on wheels, driven by a threaded shaft) enabling three tests on the system's rod:

- **Buckling test:** axial compression of the rod between jaws.
- **Torsion test:** rod clamped in place, torque applied via an auxiliary rod.
- **Bending test:** movable jaw repositioned, perpendicular force applied.

### Simulations

Von Mises stress and safety factor studies on the rod under compression and torsion, performed with **SolidWorks SimulationXpress**.
